Popular Locations to visit in Jamaica

Glistening Waters

Discover the magic of Jamaica’s famous bioluminescent lagoon, where the water comes alive with a beautiful blue-green glow after dark. Take a boat ride across the calm waters and watch as every movement creates sparkling trails beneath you.

Dunns River Falls

Explore one of Jamaica’s most iconic natural attractions, where cascading waterfalls flow over smooth limestone terraces surrounded by lush tropical scenery. Climb the falls alongside your guide, cool off in the refreshing pools, and take in the beautiful landscape as the water winds its way toward the Caribbean Sea.

Green Groto Cave

Journey beneath the island’s surface and explore a fascinating network of limestone caves filled with unique rock formations, stalactites, and underground chambers. Wander through winding passageways while learning about the caves’ geological history and their significance throughout Jamaica’s past.

Bob Marley Museum

Step into the former home of reggae legend Bob Marley and explore the place where he lived, created music, and shared his message with the world. Discover original memorabilia, photographs, recordings, and personal belongings while learning about his life, music, and lasting influence on Jamaican culture.

Port Royal

Explore the fascinating pirate-era history of this historic coastal town, once known as one of the Caribbean’s most notorious and prosperous ports. Visit historic sites, old fortifications, and remnants of the city while learning about its rise as a major trading hub and its dramatic destruction following the 1692 earthquake.

Blue Mountain

Journey through Jamaica’s scenic mountain range, home to lush forests, cool temperatures, winding roads, and the renowned Jamaica Blue Mountain Coffee. Visit local coffee farms, take in sweeping views of the countryside, and experience the peaceful atmosphere of one of the island’s most beautiful natural regions.

Mystic Mountain

Exerience the beauty of Jamaica’s tropical rainforest with panoramic views stretching across the coastline. Ride the Rainforest Sky Explorer through the treetops, take a thrilling ride on the bobsled track, or relax while taking in the surrounding mountain scenery.

Blue Lagoon

Relax beside the striking turquoise waters of this natural lagoon, surrounded by lush greenery and tropical scenery. Take a refreshing swim, glide across the calm waters by boat or raft, and enjoy the peaceful atmosphere of one of Portland’s most scenic natural attractions.
